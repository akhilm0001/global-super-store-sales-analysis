# Global Super Store Sales Analysis (2019–2023)

An end-to-end retail analytics project using **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), SQL, and Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, profitability, product trends, customer behavior, and regional performance.

## 1. Project Overview

Retail datasets contain valuable information, but raw transaction records can be difficult to interpret. This project analyzes retail sales data and presents the results through interactive Power BI dashboards.

The objective is to identify business trends, compare performance across products and regions, understand customer purchasing patterns, and communicate findings that support data-driven decisions.

## 2. Dataset Used

- **Dataset Source:** Kaggle — Indian Retail Store Data
- **Period Covered:** 2019–2023
- **Dataset Size:** 100,000 rows
- **Data Includes:** Sales, profit, products, categories, customers, geography, and shipping information.
- **Dataset Link:** [Indian Retail Store Data — Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/abuhumzakhan/store-data/data)

Refer to the Kaggle page for the original dataset, description, and usage terms.

## 3. Business Questions and KPIs

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Sales:** Overall sales revenue.
- **Total Profit:** Overall profit generated.
- **Total Orders:** Number of orders.
- **Total Customers:** Number of customers represented in the analysis.
- **Profit Margin:** Profit as a percentage of sales.

### Business Questions

- How do total sales and profit change over time?
- Which product categories and sub-categories generate the most sales and profit?
- Which products are the top and bottom performers based on the selected metric?
- Which regions and states contribute most to sales and profit?
- How does order quantity vary across product categories?
- Which customer age groups contribute the largest share of sales?
- Are there periods or categories where performance could be improved?

## 6. Key Insights

The project presentation reports the following results:

- **Total Sales:** ₹2.51 billion
- **Total Profit:** ₹375.53 million
- **Profit Margin:** 14.97%
- **Highest-Sales Quarter:** Q3 — approximately ₹632.39 million
- **Highest-Sales State:** Rajasthan — approximately ₹255.85 million
- **Improvement Opportunity:** Furniture was identified as a category with potential for improvement.

*These figures reflect the reported project results and should be validated against the final dashboard before being used for business decisions.*
